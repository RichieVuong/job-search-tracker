The simple things, done well

A little less to keep in your head.

Job hunting already takes enough energy. Keeping track of it should be the easy part.

Add it and get on with your day

Save the company and role after you apply. Your application starts as Applied, so there’s no extra setup to slow you down.

Know where things stand

Update each stage as you hear back, from online assessments and interviews to offers. Search by company or filter by stage when you need a quick look.

See the progress you’re making

Check your application count, daily goal, and weekly activity. Small steps can be easy to overlook—give yourself a moment to see them.

Why I built this

From one job seeker
to another.

I started building Application Tracker while looking for jobs myself. I wanted a simple way to keep track of where I’d applied and what stage each application was at.

The idea was straightforward: add an application, update it when something changes, and have one place to come back to. That’s what I wanted this tracker to be—a small, useful tool that makes the job search feel a little more organized.

Built from a need I had. Hopefully useful for you, too.
